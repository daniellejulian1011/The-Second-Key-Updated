# THE SECOND KEY — ROUTES & SPOILERS

## A useful first night

Start on Easy to learn the layout. Answer the phone in the living room, then use the phone again to call April. Close, lock and barricade the kitchen back door while you still have time. The copy can defeat the lock; the barricade buys additional time.

Use the stairs in the foyer to reach the landing. Pick up the camera on your bedroom desk. The repair invoice is in your parents’ bedroom. Return to the kitchen to find the muddy mark and the drawer photograph. The toolbox in the garage contains the duplicate key. The basement storage area holds the observation cassette.

Call 911 and give your full address and Nathaniel’s name. Hide before he sees you, or keep moving and use the camera flash when you have a clear line of sight. A radio in the dining room can pull him away from your route. Do not enter a closet in front of him: he remembers what he saw.

## The eight outcomes

**01 — NO MORE KEYS.** Collect at least four clues, including the repair invoice or duplicate key. Give Ortiz the full address and Nathaniel’s name. Survive the response timer. Good evidence turns his arrival into an arrest.

**02 — BLUE LIGHTS.** Call the police and survive until they arrive without the complete evidence needed for the best ending.

**03 — THE NEIGHBOR’S PORCH.** Call April so she alerts the neighbors. Open the front door and cross to the front-left edge of the yard. Interact with the neighbor’s porch when Nathaniel is more than three metres away.

**04 — THE BACK GATE.** Take the storm-cellar key in the basement. Open the cellar route and reach the far back-right gate. Interact when the hunter is more than three metres away. The back-gate route only resolves after the cellar route is opened.

**05 — ONE LAST MESSAGE.** Find at least two clues. Use the basement recorder, then leave the cassette in the front mailbox. If Nathaniel catches you afterward, your evidence still reaches April and Ortiz.

**06 — DINNER FOR TWO.** After the first call, face the closed back door while Nathaniel remains outside. Choose SPEAK THROUGH THE BACK DOOR, then AGREE TO MEET HIM FOR DINNER. If he reaches you, this capture has its own ending.

**07 — YOU LET HIM IN.** Use the same conversation, then OPEN THE DOOR FOR HIM. The door opens and Nathaniel physically approaches. If he reaches you, the ending reflects the boundary you surrendered.

**08 — THE HOLLOW RING.** A capture before rescue or a completed escape route, without the recorded-message or voluntary-entry branches.

## What the house tells you

Evelyn’s diary places the first encounter at Redbird Video on August 15, 1990. The service invoice places Nathaniel inside the Price home on September 4. Gloria’s message explains why the family trusted him with the spare key. The toolbox exposes the duplicate. The photograph of Evelyn sleeping proves that tonight is not his first unauthorized visit. His observation tape reveals how he monitored the household and left a utility access unsecured.

The first key belongs to the family. The second belongs to someone who believes he has a right to enter. His imagined relationship never changes Evelyn’s right to refuse him.
